# Calculator

A calculator for Android. No account, no tracking, no ads.

- Exact results: 1÷3×3 is 1, √2×√2 is 2, sin 30° is 0.5. Digits are cut,
  never rounded, and unfold as far as you want.
- The result as you type, the exact form under it (2/3, 2√2, √2/2).
- Edit anywhere in the expression; percentages as people mean them
  (100+10% is 110).
- Scientific keys on a band that unfolds, all at once sideways and on
  tablets; degrees or radians, inverse functions.
- History by day, sealed on the phone: take back an expression or a
  result, still exact. Memory (M+, M−, MR).
- Converter: length, area, volume, mass, temperature, speed, time, data,
  pressure, energy, power, fuel economy, angle, and every currency, with
  the day's rates kept for use offline. Type an expression as the amount.
- Sheets: lines of words and numbers worked out as you type, names
  (rent = 800), labels (Food: 320.50), totals; sealed on the phone.
- Numbers written the way your language writes them.
- A Quick Settings tile, icon shortcuts, a hardware keyboard.
